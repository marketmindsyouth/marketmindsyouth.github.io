HOW MARKET MINDS CAME TO BE

Market Minds began with a simple observation: financial habits are inherited, not taught. We built a student-led space to make money skills free, approachable, and grounded in real community.

You can search stock definitions anywhere. What was missing was a real community to learn together.

BROOKLINE, MA • FOUNDED 2024

THE SPARK

Growing up, we noticed how differently people approached money based on where they lived or what was talked about at dinner. Some peers grew up in homes where financial choices were rarely discussed, while others faced daily economic stress where long-term saving or investing was never an option.

OUR FOCUS: COMMUNITY FIRST

Financial education shouldn't feel like a high-stress competition where you're expected to know complex terms on day one. We created Market Minds to build a supportive peer community where any student can sit down, ask fundamental questions without judgment, and apply real-world money choices together.

MEET THE FOUNDER

ANNIE SHI

FOUNDER & PRESIDENT

MASSACHUSETTS

Annie founded Market Minds in 2024 in Room 359 at Brookline High School after noticing how unevenly financial habits were passed down among students.

She wanted to eliminate the intimidation around finance by building an open community where peers learn together. Today, Annie guides Market Minds’ national initiatives, workshop programs, and chapter outreach while keeping the focus on accessible, student-driven education.
