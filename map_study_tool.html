⚠️ Tricky AP Boundaries & Cheat Sheet

🏜️ The Saharan Divide

While some regions are grouped by culture, others are based on physiographic features. The Sahara Desert serves as the strict dividing line separating North Africa from Sub-Saharan Africa.

🇦🇲 Armenia and Azerbaijan

Geographers debate regional definitions! Because both countries were formerly parts of the Soviet Union, some geographers place them in Central Asia, while others group them into the Middle East.

🌎 Latin America vs. North America

Look closely at the APHG Closer Look map: Mexico and the Caribbean are grouped down into Latin America, leaving only the United States and Canada in the "North America" region.

🥶 The Russian Federation

Russia is so massive that it is categorized as its own distinct overarching region north of Europe and Asia. Siberia is identified as its specific eastern sub-region.
