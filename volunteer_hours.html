Crucial Guideline

THE INTERACTIVITY RULE

To get your hours approved, your resource MUST be interactive. We do not want boring textbooks. We want to see students making choices, solving problems, and applying knowledge. Here is what we expect:

For Writers

MAKE THEM THINK

Don't just list definitions. Frame your concepts around real people. Include a mini "Case Study" (e.g., Meet Sarah, she has $500 to invest...), build a "Myth vs. Fact" section, or write out a scenario where the reader has to pause and make a financial choice.

For Designers

CREATE USABLE TOOLS

Don't just make a poster full of text. Design something chapters can actually use! Think printable budgeting templates, a "Financial Bingo" card, an intuitive flowchart, or a fill-in-the-blank worksheet that students can complete during meetings.

For Video Creators

DRIVE ENGAGEMENT

Ask a question at the very beginning of the video for the viewer to answer in their head. Do a "Put a finger down if..." financial edition, or present a money dilemma and ask the audience to vote in the comments. Keep the energy high!
