The Founder

Growing up, I noticed that students approached money completely differently based on their backgrounds. Some talked about investing at dinner; others were focused on immediate needs. Financial habits were being inherited, not taught.

I founded Market Minds to fix that. What started in Room 359 at Brookline HS has expanded into a 501(c)(3) nonprofit operating across 9+ states and 3+ countries, focusing on peer-to-peer financial literacy.

The Researcher

I love figuring out the logic hidden inside complex systems. During my eight months as Research Staff for Harvard Business School, I authored a full MBA teaching case on the globalization of K-pop for the Strategy unit.

Before that, I interned at Global Delta Capital, looking into everything from global lithium battery markets to representation gaps in open source AI models. I love taking raw data and turning it into a narrative that actually explains human behavior.

The Athlete

Competitive high school rower. I train year-round, dedicating 24+ hours a week to practices, independent training, and weekend regattas. Rowing demands ultimate discipline. It is the shared relentlessness of pushing limits alongside a team that shapes my mindset in everything else I do.

The Journalist

As the Managing Editor of The Cypress, I oversee production, assign stories, edit articles, and manage a team of student writers for our school's newspaper. Journalism is how I actively report on and understand the world around me.

Beyond print, I was selected for the Whipple Brookline Life Podcast Program, a journalism initiative where I researched, interviewed, and produced a narrative podcast documenting the people and stories within our community.
