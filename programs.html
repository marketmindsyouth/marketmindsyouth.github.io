HIGH SCHOOL CHAPTER PROGRAM

BUILDING FINANCIAL CLUBS THAT STUDENTS ACTUALLY WANT TO JOIN

Our high school chapters are built to be approachable and community-centered from day one. While student chapter leaders receive comprehensive support and curriculum resources, general members don't need any prior background in stocks, economics, or business to join and participate.

WHAT A CHAPTER MEETING LOOKS LIKE

  • • Investing & Market Simulations: Practice stock-picking strategies in risk-free interactive challenges.
  • • Finance Trivia & Games: Friendly competitions breaking down real-world financial headlines.
  • • Open Discussion: Asking fundamental questions without feeling judged.

YOUTH FINANCIAL LITERACY INITIATIVE

FREE WORKSHOPS FOR ELEMENTARY & MIDDLE SCHOOLS

We believe financial literacy should begin early before complex money choices happen. We partner with schools, public libraries, and youth centers to deliver interactive, zero-cost workshops that build early financial confidence.

CONNECT SERIES GUEST SPEAKER PROGRAM

REAL CAREER INSIGHTS FROM REAL PROFESSIONALS

The Connect Series bridges the gap between high school students and professionals across finance, business, marketing, and entrepreneurship. Students gain direct access to candid career advice and Q&A sessions.

FOR SPEAKERS & PARTNERS
