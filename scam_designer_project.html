Scam Designer Project

To catch a scammer, you have to learn how to think like one! Your mission is to use what you've learned today to design your very own mock-scam. Be creative, be sneaky, but make sure to leave "Red Flags" behind so a smart student could spot it!

The Blueprint

Every successful scam needs three things: A fake identity, a hook (something to make them panic or get excited), and a trap (what you want them to click).

Need inspiration? Check these examples:

✉️ Example 1: The Urgent Panic
Sender: Security_Alert@Paypel-Support.com
Message: "Your account has been locked due to suspicious activity. Click here within 24 hours to verify your identity or your account will be deleted!"
The Red Flags: Paypel is misspelled, and they are creating fake panic with a 24-hour countdown.
💬 Example 2: The Too-Good-To-Be-True
Sender: @MrBeast_Giveaway_Real99 (Instagram)
Message: "CONGRATS! You won $10,000 in my latest video! Send $50 on CashApp to cover the shipping fee and I will send the money right away."
The Red Flags: Real giveaways never ask YOU to pay money to get your prize. The username is also fake.
