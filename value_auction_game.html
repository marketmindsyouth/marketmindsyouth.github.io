The Value Auction

Welcome to the high-stakes auction house! You have a starting budget of $1,000 to prepare for the upcoming school year.

📜 THE RULES OF THE AUCTION:
  • Sealed Bids: You only get ONE chance to bid. You must type in the absolute maximum you are willing to pay.
  • Winner Pays Their Bid: If your bid beats the computer's secret bid, you win! But be careful—you will pay exactly what you typed. If you bid $300 on a $50 item, you lose $250 in value!
  • Needs vs. Wants: Some items you truly need, others are just for fun. Make sure you don't blow your budget on Wants before you secure your Needs!
